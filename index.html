01 / THE SETTING

A WORLD THAT
DOES NOT WAIT FOR YOU.

Varethia exists beyond the player's view. Kingdoms bargain and betray, merchants trade, armies march, and ordinary people live their lives whether you are watching or not.

THE KNOWN WORLD
✦
A VAST, CONNECTED WORLD

Nine continents. Eighty-three kingdoms.

Independent settlements, frontier territories, ancient ruins, magical forests, oceans, deserts and cursed lands create a world larger than any one person's ambitions.

ROYAL HOUSES

Power has a price.

Rulers recruit the gifted. Rivals conspire. Assassins accept contracts. Institutions may protect, study or exploit the powerful.

LOST CIVILIZATIONS

History is buried.

Forgotten civilizations left behind forbidden knowledge, sealed places and mysteries that can change the balance of power.
